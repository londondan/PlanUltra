  1. 1

    A link you can text

    Send it to your crew. It opens on any phone, with no app or account.

  2. 2

    Directions to the parking

    Each crew stop links to where to park, not just the aid station, with notes on the lot, the walk in, or the shuttle.

  3. 3

    A printed copy for when there's no signal

    Print it before you leave. Each stop has a QR code that opens the parking spot in your maps app.

    Coordinates are printed under each code, so an offline map can still find the spot.

Who made this

I built PlanUltra after a DNF at Grindstone 100, wanting to do better by my crew next time. Race websites list the aid stations. Crews need to know where to park, how to get in, and which stops they can skip.

This is a hobby project, not a startup. It's free, there are no ads or accounts, and the code is on GitHub. It's also an example of my product work, so if you want to talk about that, find me on LinkedIn.

If you spot a wrong parking location, email me and I'll fix it.

PlanUltra is free and open source.
