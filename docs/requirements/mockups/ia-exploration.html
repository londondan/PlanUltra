Race Dashboard Layout Options

Four structural approaches for organising course data, plan inputs, and packing across the Plan and Prep/Pack views

Concept A
Scrolling Single Page with Collapsible Sections
Everything lives on one page in a deliberate vertical order. Sections the user rarely revisits (map) start collapsed. The elevation graph is always visible as a compact sticky strip. Section planning is the main body. Packing Plan is prominent at the bottom — a natural destination after planning is done.
⚠ The map is collapsed by default after first visit. Elevation is pinned as a thin strip. Packing plan scrolls into view at the bottom once planning is complete.

  • User wants one continuous surface to scroll through
  • No mode-switching friction
  • Packing plan is a natural "reward" at the end

Watch out for…

  • No distinct "prep mode" vs "plan mode" context
Concept B
Job-Aware Tabs: Plan · Pack · Crew
Three tabs, each matching a distinct runner job. Plan (weeks out): course reference + section inputs. Pack (week before): packing plan front-and-center, sections collapsed for review. Crew: a published view with a share link. Elevation lives in a compact sticky banner across all tabs.
💡 The current "Plan" tab splits into three context-aware tabs. This matches how the runner actually uses the app over time — the same data, different views.

  • Different jobs deserve different visual hierarchies
  • Pack tab can re-order content around packing, not planning
  • Crew tab can be published independently

Watch out for…

Concept C
Persistent Sidebar with Scrollable Main Content
The course is a fixed reference — it doesn't change. Pin it to the left as a persistent sidebar: station list, elevation thumbnail, and weather summary. The right panel is the working area, scrolling through section inputs then packing plan. The runner always knows where they are on the course while editing.
💡 This is the most common layout in logistics apps (e.g., Google Maps, route planners) where the map/reference is persistent. Works best on wider screens.

Concept D
Course Strip + Sequential Focus Sections
The elevation profile is permanently pinned at the top as a clickable strip — clicking a station on the strip jumps to that section card. Below it, one active section is in "focus" at a time, showing full plan inputs and the packing card for that station simultaneously. A horizontal stepper lets you move through sections. Weather and map collapse into a single "Course info" accordion. This mimics how runners actually think: one section at a time.
💡 Key insight: you rarely need to see all sections at once. You're always thinking about one leg at a time. This design collapses the vertical list into a horizontal stepper.
