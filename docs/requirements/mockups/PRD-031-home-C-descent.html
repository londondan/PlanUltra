Built after a bad day at Grindstone.

I'm Dan James, a product manager and ultrarunner. After a DNF at Grindstone 100, I wanted to do better by my crew. Race websites list the aid stations. Crews need to know where to park and which stops to skip.

It's a hobby project: free, no ads, no accounts, and the code is on GitHub. If a parking spot is wrong, email me. For the product side, I'm on LinkedIn.
