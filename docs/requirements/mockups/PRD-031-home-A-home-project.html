What your crew gets

Here's the top of a sheet for a made-up race:

  1. A link you can text. Opens on any phone. They don't need an app or an account.
  2. Directions to the parking, not just the aid station, with a note on the lot, the walk in, or the shuttle.
  3. A printout for when there's no signal. Each stop has a QR code for the maps app, with the coordinates printed underneath in case the phone can't load anything.

I'm Dan. I'm a product manager in Charlotte and I run ultras. I started this after a DNF at Grindstone 100. The race website listed the aid stations, but my crew needed to know where to park and which stops they could skip.

It's a hobby project. It's free, there are no ads or accounts, and the code is on GitHub. If a parking spot is wrong, email me and I'll fix it.

I'm also on LinkedIn if you want to talk about the product side.
