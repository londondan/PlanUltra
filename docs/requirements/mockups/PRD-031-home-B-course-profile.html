Made by a runner, for his crew

I'm Dan James, a product manager and ultrarunner. I built PlanUltra after a DNF at Grindstone 100. The race website listed the aid stations, but my crew needed to know where to park and which stops to skip.

It's a hobby project: free, no ads, no accounts, and open source. Spot a wrong parking location? Email me and I'll fix it.
