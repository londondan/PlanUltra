CRUEL JEWEL 100
Race Packet 2025

Race Date: Friday, May 16, 2025
Start Time: 12:00 PM (Noon)
Location: Camp Morganton, Blairsville, GA
Distance: 104.3 miles
Time Limit: 42 hours

The Cruel Jewel 100 is a 104.3-mile loop through the Chattahoochee National
Forest of the North Georgia Mountains, featuring 20 aid stations and over
20,000 feet of elevation gain.

CREW AND PACER INFORMATION
--------------------------
Crew may access runners at any aid station marked "Yes" above.
Pacers are permitted starting at Neels Gap (mile 34.1) and any subsequent crew station.
Only one pacer allowed on course at a time.

Parking at Vogel State Park: Day use parking lot off Hwy 129, 0.5 miles north of
the aid station. Walk the connector trail to the aid station.

Parking at Neels Gap: Use the Mountain Crossings outfitter parking lot. The aid
station is at the building — no walking required.

Parking at Tesnatee Gap: Limited pull-off space on Richard Russell Hwy. Arrive early.

Parking at Unicoi Gap: Dedicated parking lot on the east side of Hwy 75 at the gap.

Parking at Dicks Creek Gap: Paved lot on US-76. Aid station is 50 meters from the
road at the trailhead.
